# parts list

this is the working bill of materials for the four-hand prototype!!!

(exact parts may change as the mechanism continues to be refined)

| component | quantity | notes |
|---|---:|---|
| raspberry pi | 1 | zero 2 w or another wifi-capable raspberry pi |
| microsd card | 1 | 16 gb minimum, 32 gb recommended, class 10 or better |
| pca9685 servo driver | 1 | 16-channel i2c pwm controller |
| jx w5513 / sw5513-6ma servo | 4 | 25t spline sail winch servo |
| 32t servo gear | 4 | mounted to servo output |
| 64t driven gear | 4 | mounted to concentric tubes |
| telescoping tubing | 1 set | nested concentric hand shafts |
| 25t servo horns | 4 | useful for gear or hub mounting |
| 5v servo power supply | 1 | prototype uses approximately 4a capacity |
| raspberry pi power supply | 1 | appropriate supply for selected pi |
| jumper wire | as needed | pca9685 and power connections |
| clock case | 1 | donor clock or custom enclosure |
| clock hands | 4 | one for each rotating tube |

## tubing

the prototype uses telescoping tubing including

- 1/16 in od
- 3/32 in od
- 1/8 in od
- 5/32 in od
- 3/16 in od
- 7/32 in od
- 1/4 in od
- 9/32 in od

not every size is necessarily required in the final four-hand assembly

## tools

helpful tools include

- rotary tool with cutting wheel
- small files or deburring tools
- ruler or calipers
- screwdriver set
- wire cutters
- hot glue gun for prototyping
- soldering equipment if permanent electrical connections are desired

## substitutions

the project does not depend on the exact donor clock

servo, gear, tubing, hub, and mounting choices may also be changed as long as each hand can rotate independently and the servo can reach all required clock positions
